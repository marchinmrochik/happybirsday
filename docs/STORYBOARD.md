# Storyboard

The current experience is a desktop-first interactive birthday episode. It uses one continuous page and one portal narrative.

## Scene 1: Interdimensional Scanner

The page opens on a dark diagnostic console. System rows appear sequentially, including a temporary Jerry-detection error and a longer correction pause. The object profile appears with the final diagnostic row.

The viewer sees Roman Borzov, age 30, birth date `14.08.1996`, and the level-30 status, then clicks the portal CTA.

## Scene 2: Portal Gun

The 3D portal gun is the interaction target. The viewer clicks the gun, not a separate HTML button.

The gun fires a beam toward the portal center. After `1.18 s`, the green portal stabilizes and the local music starts. The viewer then clicks the portal itself.

## Scene 3: Travel

A short green dimensional transition runs for `2.85 s`. It uses a direct tunnel effect without large foreground shapes or additional portals.

## Scene 4: Playable Garage Laboratory

The viewer controls a third-person character inside the supplied garage environment.

- `WASD` or arrows move.
- `Shift` runs.
- `Space` jumps.
- mouse/touch rotates the horizontal view.
- `E` or `Enter` uses the single focused object.
- `F` kicks a focused sports ball.

Memory photos are distributed across the room and open a large modal. A floor mirror opens the live character customizer. Football and basketball props can be picked up, thrown, or kicked. Furniture, benches, cabinets, and the mirror block movement.

The chemistry station on the workbench starts the analysis.

## Scene 5: Analysis HUD

A full-screen comic sci-fi HUD reveals:

- name, age, birth date, status, level, rarity, and compatibility;
- a framed Roman portrait;
- seven achievement cards;
- a final system result banner.

The reveal completes after `7.6 s`. The full screen remains available for reading before the story advances at `18.8 s`.

## Scene 6: Results Dialogue

The portal returns. Rick and Morty model assets appear to the left and right of a centered results panel. Structured dialogue reveals line by line with analysis, status values, a level-up callout, and wishes for the next season.

The dialogue remains for `11.8 s`.

## Scene 7: Birthday Epilogue

The portal and side models close away. A final HUD panel shows:

- `Добро пожаловать в` as the smaller lead;
- `30-й сезон жизни!` as the large title;
- `С днем рождения!` as the exact greeting;
- the loaded-season status.

A transparent final character appears over the existing background with the closing speech bubble.
